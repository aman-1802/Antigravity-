## Spotlight: 🧠 AI Material Price Spike
- **Link**: [🧠 AI Material Price Spike](https://www.scmp.com/tech/tech-trends/article/3364327/next-silicon-ai-data-centre-material-faces-price-spike-amid-china-supply-crunch?utm_source=rss_feed)

The AI boom is driving an unprecedented demand for indium phosphide (InP), a critical material for optical modules, leading to soaring prices amid supply constraints from China.

As the primary producer, China's supply crunch is causing significant price hikes, which could impact the cost of AI infrastructure and data center operations globally.

This situation underscores the fragility of supply chains in the tech sector and highlights the need for diversification in sourcing critical materials.
